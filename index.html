About Meal Plan

Your simple nutrition tracking companion
ABOUT THE PROJECT

Small steps toward mindful eating.

Meal Plan helps you record foods, review estimated nutrients, and set personal daily targets in one simple place.

🌱Track · Reflect · Improve
🍽️

Food diary

Record foods and servings to calculate estimated calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fat.

🎯

Daily goals

Set your own nutrition targets and keep a dated, timed history of saved goals.

🔒

Local storage

Entries are saved in this browser on this device. They are not synced to an online account.

🧪

Prototype status

A browser-based prototype with a built-in table of 30 foods in ten groups. A TF-IDF and Logistic Regression classifier is trained in your browser and retrained whenever the food list changes. There is no server backend.

Nutrition values are illustrative estimates and may vary by brand, serving size, and preparation. This tool is for general tracking, not medical advice.
